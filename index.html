FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding consultation hours, maternity care, and hospital visits.

What are your consultation timings? +
The hospital and emergency care are available 24 × 7. Our OPD consultation runs Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. You can call or WhatsApp us to book an appointment.
Do you handle high-risk pregnancies? +
Yes. Dr. Shilpi Singh specialises in high-risk pregnancy care, offering close monitoring and dedicated support to keep both mother and baby safe.
Can I consult for PCOS, PCOD, or irregular periods? +
Absolutely. We provide diagnosis and personalised management plans for PCOS/PCOD, as well as care for irregular, painful, or heavy periods.
Do you offer fertility and infertility consultations? +
Yes, we offer supportive evaluation and consultation for fertility concerns in a comfortable and confidential setting.
Do you offer painless delivery options for childbirth? +
Yes, we support painless deliveries using epidural analgesia. Dr. Shilpi Singh will discuss all pain relief options during your regular check-ups to help make your birth experience as calm and comfortable as possible.
What advanced treatments are available for gynecological conditions? +
We offer laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery for ovarian cysts, uterine fibroids, and other conditions. Laparoscopic surgery offers key benefits: significantly less pain, tiny incisions, and a much faster recovery so you can get back to your routine quickly.
How does the hospital support women dealing with hormonal issues like PCOS? +
We believe in a personalized and supportive approach. Beyond medication, we help you manage female hormonal disorders through customized lifestyle adjustments, nutritional advice, and close monitoring to restore natural balance.
Where is Stackly Women's Health Center located, and how do we reach it? +
Our hospital is located at MMR Complex, Periyakollappatty, Chinna Thirupathi (near Chinna Muniyappan Kovil), Salem, TN 636008. We are situated at a highly accessible location with convenient transport links. If you need any directions, feel free to call our reception at +91 70107 92745.
