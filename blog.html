Doctor FAQ

Frequently Asked Health Questions

Is epidural analgesia safe for both mother and child during normal delivery? +
Yes, modern low-dose walking epidurals provide substantial pain relief without causing systemic sedation to the infant or stalling physiological cervical dilation. Our anesthesiology team maintains continuous telemetry throughout labor.
How quickly can a patient return to normal work after laparoscopic cystectomy? +
Because incisions are only 3 to 5mm in length, abdominal muscle disruption is minimal. Most patients are discharged within 24 to 48 hours and resume light routine activities within 4 to 6 days.
What dietary modifications are most effective for insulin resistance in PCOS? +
We recommend prioritizing complex unrefined carbohydrates with high fiber, combining meals with healthy fats and lean protein, and avoiding liquid sugars to prevent postprandial insulin surges.
