OPD FAQ

Common Questions Regarding Hospital Visits

What are your regular OPD consultation hours? +
Emergency maternity care is active 24 × 7. Our OPD consultation runs Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM (Morning Session) and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM (Evening Session).
Do I need an advance appointment for an emergency labor visit? +
No advance appointment is needed for emergencies or active labor. You can come directly to our casualty/maternity emergency wing at any hour. If possible, call us at +91 70107 92745 while on the way so our obstetric team is fully scrubbed and ready.
Is wheelchair assistance available at the hospital entrance? +
Yes, our hospital entrance features an immediate ramp, standby wheelchairs, and trained patient assistance staff to assist expectant mothers or post-operative patients directly to their consultation chamber.
