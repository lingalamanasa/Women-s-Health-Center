Infection Control

Stringent NABH Protocols and Zero-Infection Standards

Hospital-acquired infections pose severe risks to vulnerable newborns and surgical patients. At Stackly Women's Health Center, we implement an uncompromising 6-stage sterilization protocol monitored through continuous biological testing:

✓ Autoclave and plasma sterilization for all surgical micro-instruments.
✓ 99.97% HEPA air filtration with positive pressure airflow in operating rooms.
✓ Dedicated Level-3 NICU scrub station and continuous UV air decontaminators.
✓ 256-Bit SSL encrypted digital health records protecting patient confidentiality.
